Eighty-two scene illustrations in this deck incorporate [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/) icons created by the OpenMoji project at HfG Schwäbisch Gmünd and its contributors.

OpenMoji artwork is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The other 12 illustrated scenes are original SVG artwork. The six photographic backgrounds are covered separately in [PHOTO-CREDITS.md](PHOTO-CREDITS.md).
